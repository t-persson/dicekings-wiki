# The Missing Riders

> [!note] Incomplete entry
> This matter is unresolved. Anyone with knowledge of the riders is encouraged to add to this entry.

**Seen:** Two [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/People/Riderless-Horses|riderless horses]] and scattered gear stood on the Triboar Trail when the goblins attacked.

**Reported by a witness:** One horse, questioned by Groot, said [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/People/Sildar-Hallwinter|Sildar]] and a dwarf had been taken into the forest. [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/People/Gundren-Rockseeker|Gundren]], the dwarf who hired the escorts, had ridden ahead with Sildar.

**Followed lead:** The escorts went north through the woods and reached the [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/Locations/Forest-Cave|Forest Cave]], where they defeated goblin lookouts. They have since explored deeper, fought goblins, wolves and a bugbear, and crossed a bridge to another goblin cave. The [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/Journal/Session-02|second journal entry]] ends as a new encounter begins there.

**Still unknown:** Neither rider's whereabouts or condition has been established. The search inside the cave is unfinished.
