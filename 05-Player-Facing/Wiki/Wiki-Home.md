# Welcome to the Wiki

A reference to the places, people and affairs of the northern Sword Coast, as known to those who travel it. Some entries come from firsthand experience; others are only what travellers have heard, and are marked as incomplete. Where something is not known, the entries say so.

**Latest news from the road:** The escort company hired to bring Gundren Rockseeker's wagon south now stands outside the [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/Locations/Forest-Cave|Forest Cave]], having killed its goblin lookouts. Skarn has announced that he means to rush inside.

## Travellers' notes
Neverwinter, the High Road and the Triboar Trail are described from the journey and common knowledge of the region. Phandalin and Barthen's Provisions are known to the escorts only through Gundren's job notice; no one in the company has yet reached them.
