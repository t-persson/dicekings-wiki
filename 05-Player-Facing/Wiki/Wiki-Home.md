# Welcome to the Wiki

A reference to the places, people and affairs of the northern Sword Coast, as known to those who travel it. Some entries come from firsthand experience; others are only what travellers have heard, and are marked as incomplete. Where something is not known, the entries say so.

> **Last confirmed campaign date:** 16 Tarsakh, 1491 DR · **Confirmed in-game days elapsed:** 3 (since departure from Neverwinter on 13 Tarsakh). The long rest in the cave advanced the in-game day by one.

## Where we left off

The den fight beyond the bridge in the [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/Locations/Forest-Cave|Forest Cave]] is over. Skarn killed both goblin bosses; Tyr and Rolph were felled but helped up. [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/People/Sildar-Hallwinter|Sildar]] survived being dropped from the ledge. The company took a long rest, reached level 2, and agreed to escort him to [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/Locations/Phandalin|Phandalin]].

After speaking with Sildar, the company left the cave to check on the wagon, oxen and horses; everything was there and okay. They headed for Phandalin and stopped just before entering. Sildar spoke about an elongated-head goblin, Cragmaw Castle and Gundren's map. [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/People/Gundren-Rockseeker|Gundren]] and the map remain missing. The containers left in the cave have not been recovered; only the broken-off piece bearing the [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/Lore/Blue-Lion-Mark|blue lion mark]] was carried away.
