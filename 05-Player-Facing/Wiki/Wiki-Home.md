# Welcome to the Wiki

A reference to the places, people and affairs of the northern Sword Coast, as known to those who travel it. Some entries come from firsthand experience; others are only what travellers have heard, and are marked as incomplete. Where something is not known, the entries say so.

> **Campaign date:** 15 Tarsakh, 1491 DR · **In-game days elapsed:** 2 (since departure from Neverwinter on 13 Tarsakh)

## Where we left off

The escort company is inside the [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/Locations/Forest-Cave|Forest Cave]], in a goblin cave beyond the bridge. Rolph scouted a goblin boss; Tyr advanced and missed with an arrow. Initiative has been rolled, but the encounter remains unresolved. Earlier, the company survived two floods, killed a bugbear, found containers bearing a [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/Lore/Blue-Lion-Mark|blue lion mark]], and took a short rest. Only Tyr's broken-off piece bearing the mark was carried away; Phandalin has not been reached.
