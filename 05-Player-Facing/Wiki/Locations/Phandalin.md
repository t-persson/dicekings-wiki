# Phandalin

> [!note] Incomplete entry
> The escorts have not yet visited Phandalin. This entry rests on Gundren's job notice; those who have walked its streets are invited to add to it.

Phandalin is a frontier mining town, the destination of [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/Affairs/Wagon-Escort-Job|a wagon escort job]] posted in Neverwinter.

## A Destination on the Road

Gundren's notice gives the route: south from Neverwinter along the High Road, then east along the Triboar Trail toward Phandalin.

The promised delivery is a wagon of provisions and mining equipment. The escorts have yet to see the town's streets or learn its daily life firsthand.

## Landmarks

- **[[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/Locations/Barthens-Provisions|Barthen's Provisions]]:** a trading post, named as the delivery address for Gundren Rockseeker's supply wagon.

## Notable happenings

The wagon bound for Phandalin leaves [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/Locations/Neverwinter|Neverwinter]] by the High Road and turns east onto the [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/Locations/Triboar-Trail|Triboar Trail]]. By the terms of the job, it is to be delivered to Barthen's Provisions, with ten gold pieces promised to each escort on safe delivery. The wagon has not yet arrived.
