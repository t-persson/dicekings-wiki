# Phandalin

> [!note] Incomplete entry
> The escorts have stopped just before entering Phandalin. This entry rests on Gundren's job notice; those who have walked its streets are invited to add to it.

Phandalin is a frontier mining town, the destination of [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/Affairs/Wagon-Escort-Job|a wagon escort job]] posted in Neverwinter.

## A Destination on the Road

Gundren's notice gives the route: south from Neverwinter along the High Road, then east along the Triboar Trail toward Phandalin.

The promised delivery is a wagon of provisions and mining equipment. The escorts have not yet entered the town or learned its daily life firsthand.

## Landmarks

- **[[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/Locations/Barthens-Provisions|Barthen's Provisions]]:** a trading post, named as the delivery address for Gundren Rockseeker's supply wagon.

## Notable happenings

The wagon bound for Phandalin leaves [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/Locations/Neverwinter|Neverwinter]] by the High Road and turns east onto the [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/Locations/Triboar-Trail|Triboar Trail]]. By the terms of the job, it is to be delivered to Barthen's Provisions, with ten gold pieces promised to each escort on safe delivery. The company has reached the town's approach but stopped just before entering; the delivery is not yet complete.
