# Forest Cave

> [!note] Incomplete entry
> The den fight is over. The escorts still do not know the cave's name, and the containers remain inside.

The Forest Cave is a hillside opening reached by following a trail north from the [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/Locations/Triboar-Trail|roadside ambush site]]. The escorts have entered it, but do not know its name.

## A Stream Beneath the Briars

Dense briars screen the opening at the foot of a wooded hill. A shallow stream slips out from beneath the hillside, its cold water moving slowly past the thickets. Beside the water, a narrow strip of dry ground leads to the entrance; the brush conceals a lookout post nearby.

## Landmarks

- **The cave mouth:** the hillside opening where the woodland trail ends and the stream emerges.
- **The guard post:** a lookout position screened by briars beside the stream, where goblins watched the entrance.
- **The wolves' chamber:** just inside the cave, where Skarn briefly intimidated the [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/People/Cave-Wolves|wolves]].
- **The cavern deeper inside:** where the escorts fought goblins and wolves after two floods surged through the passage.
- **The bugbear's room:** where the [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/People/Cave-Bugbear|bugbear]] was killed, marked containers were searched, and the escorts took a short rest.
- **The bridge:** crossed after the short rest to reach the goblin den.
- **The goblin den:** a room with stairs to a higher ledge, where the escorts fought two goblin bosses and found Sildar. They took a long rest here.

## Notable happenings

The trail north from the road runs through woods and past a pit trap, which Rolph spotted in time. [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/People/Goblins|Goblin lookouts]] ambushed the escorts while they tried to look inside the cave, and the escorts defeated them.

Inside, the escorts survived two floods and fought goblins and wolves. KraziKatt killed the bugbear with *Guiding Bolt*. Tyr was knocked down in that fight, then stabilized by KraziKatt and healed by Groot. One goblin escaped down a trash chute.

The searched containers bore a [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/Lore/Blue-Lion-Mark|blue lion mark]]. Tyr took a broken-off piece bearing the mark; the containers stayed behind.

After a short rest, the party crossed the bridge. The [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/Journal/Session-02|second journal entry]] ends at the start of the den encounter.

In the den, Tyr and Rolph were felled but helped up. A goblin boss tried to parley while holding [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/People/Sildar-Hallwinter|Sildar]] over the ledge; when ignored, he dropped Sildar, who nearly died but survived. Skarn killed both bosses. The company took a long rest there and reached level 2, as recorded in the [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/Journal/Session-03|third journal entry]].

Sildar asked for an escort to Phandalin, and the party agreed. After speaking with him, the escorts left the cave to check on the wagon and animals. The containers have not been recovered. [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/Events/Missing-Riders|Gundren remains missing]].
