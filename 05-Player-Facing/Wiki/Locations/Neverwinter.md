# Neverwinter

Neverwinter is the city where [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/People/Gundren-Rockseeker|Gundren Rockseeker]] took on escorts for his wagon bound for Phandalin.

## The City of Skilled Hands
Neverwinter's old name, the City of Skilled Hands, is an ambition as well as a boast. Craftsfolk are returning, workshops are opening, and newcomers seek work as laborers or apprentices. Growing trade brings employment guarding caravans on the High Road.

Mount Hotenow warms the river through the city, but rebuilding does not guarantee an easy living. Inns and shops sometimes run short of food as arrivals outstrip their supplies; farmers and provision merchants find a ready market here. Small shrines to many gods stand throughout the city, alongside its workshops and trade.

## Landmarks
- **The Three Bridges:** the Dolphin, the Winged Wyvern and the Sleeping Dragon.
- **Protector's Enclave:** the district around the Hall of Justice, from which Lord Protector Dagult Neverember's influence extends.
- **The docks:** Neverwinter's growing shipping trade brings goods by sea; the Driftwood Tavern stands near the waterfront.
- **The Moonstone Mask:** an inn named alongside the Driftwood Tavern among the posting places for Gundren's notice.
- **South Gate and Caravan Grounds:** the departure point specified in Gundren's job notice.

## Recent events
In Neverwinter, Gundren met the escorts and they accepted [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/Affairs/Wagon-Escort-Job|his wagon escort job]]. The company then departed south along the [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/Locations/High-Road|High Road]], bound for [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/Locations/Phandalin|Phandalin]].
