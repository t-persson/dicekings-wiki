# High Road

The High Road is the coastal trade route south from [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/Locations/Neverwinter|Neverwinter]], and the first leg of the wagon journey to Phandalin.

## The Coastward Trade Road

Running along the Sword Coast toward Waterdeep, the High Road connects Neverwinter's busy trade to the lands farther south. Its restored route carries caravans and offers work for their escorts. For a wagon team, the broad regional connection becomes something slower and more immediate: the creak of a load and the steady pace of oxen.

As elsewhere in Faerûn, a traveller may ask Beshaba to withhold misfortune before crossing a rickety bridge. Such appeals need not mean taking the goddess as a patron.

## Landmarks

- **The Triboar Trail junction:** the eastward turn toward Phandalin, where wagons leave the coastal route behind.

## Notable happenings

Gundren Rockseeker's supply wagon travelled south from Neverwinter along the road before turning east onto the [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/Locations/Triboar-Trail|Triboar Trail]]. During the two-day journey, Rolph drove the wagon, Skarn started a game in the back that the others joined, and watches were kept at night. A traveller's account of the trip is kept in [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/Journal/Session-01|the journal]].
