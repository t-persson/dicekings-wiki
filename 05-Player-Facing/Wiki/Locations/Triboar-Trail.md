# Triboar Trail

The Triboar Trail is the eastbound route from the [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/Locations/High-Road|High Road]] toward [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/Locations/Phandalin|Phandalin]], where a wagon company was ambushed by goblins.

## Between Forest and Foothills

The trail crosses a landscape of foothills and rolling, sparsely wooded plains, with Neverwinter Wood to the north and the Sword Mountains to the south. Open stretches give way to close woodland. At one bend, steep banks and tangled brush crowd the road and hide what lies just beyond a wagon's path.

Across the North, a road between settlements is no assurance of safety: bandits and dangerous creatures haunt the intervening wilds. Merchants hire guards rather than trust their cargo to the road alone.

## Landmarks

- **The wooded bend:** the ambush site, where two living, riderless horses were found among scattered gear.
- **The northbound woodland trail:** a path leading away from the road toward the [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/Locations/Forest-Cave|Forest Cave]].

## Notable happenings

A few hours after turning east from the High Road, Gundren Rockseeker's escorts found two [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/People/Riderless-Horses|riderless horses]] and scattered gear at the bend. Four [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/People/Goblins|goblins]] attacked. Three fell, and Rolph was the only one of the escorts substantially hurt. The fourth goblin fled toward the cave.

Skarn chased it, sprang a rope snare, and was hoisted upside down into a tree. He cut himself free, hurt himself slightly, and vented his anger on a tree stump. The goblin escaped into the woods.

Back at the road, Groot Thornmark cast *Speak with Animals* to question the horses. One ignored him; the other, panicking, said that Sildar and a dwarf had been taken into the forest. The escorts tethered the horses and oxen to trees and followed the trail north. Rolph spotted a pit trap in time for everyone to avoid it, and they reached the Forest Cave. The horse's report is a lead only; it does not confirm where the riders are now.
