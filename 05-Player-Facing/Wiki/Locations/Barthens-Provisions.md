# Barthen's Provisions

> [!note] Incomplete entry
> The escorts know this address only from Gundren's job notice and have not visited it. Anyone who has stood in its doorway is encouraged to add to this entry.

Barthen's Provisions is a trading post in [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/Locations/Phandalin|Phandalin]], named as the delivery address for a supply wagon sent out of Neverwinter.

## Provisions for the Frontier

Gundren's notice names Barthen's as the destination for flour, salted pork, ale, mining tools, lanterns and lamp oil. It says nothing of the shop's usual stock or hours.

Throughout Faerûn, supply caravans carry news as well as merchandise. An account passed along the road is not necessarily a firsthand one.

## Notable happenings

By [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/People/Gundren-Rockseeker|Gundren Rockseeker's]] account, his supply wagon is to be brought here, and the escorts hired to bring it are promised ten gold pieces each on safe delivery, under the terms of [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/Affairs/Wagon-Escort-Job|the escort job]]. The escorts have not met the shopkeeper, and the wagon has not arrived.
