# Journal: The Road Out of Neverwinter

*Written by one of the hired escorts, from the road.*

**In-world date:** 13–15 Tarsakh, 1491 DR

In [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/Locations/Neverwinter|Neverwinter]] we met [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/People/Gundren-Rockseeker|Gundren Rockseeker]] and agreed to see his supply wagon to Phandalin. Rolph took the reins. Skarn started a game of dice in the back, and before long the rest of us had joined in. Two days went by on the road, and we kept watches at night.

A few hours after the turn onto the [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/Locations/Triboar-Trail|Triboar Trail]], we came upon two riderless horses and scattered gear at a wooded bend. Then four goblins attacked. We beat down three; Rolph took the worst of the hurt. The fourth ran into the woods.

Skarn went after it and was hauled upside down by a rope snare. He cut himself free, took a little more hurt, and vented his temper on a tree stump. The goblin got away.

Back at the road, Groot Thornmark spoke with the [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/People/Riderless-Horses|horses]]. One would not answer. The other told him that Sildar and a dwarf had been taken into the forest. We tethered the horses and oxen to trees and headed north. Rolph spotted a pit trap before any of us fell in.

At the [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/Locations/Forest-Cave|cave]], goblin lookouts attacked while we tried to peer inside. We beat them as well. Skarn then announced that he would run into the cave.

**As this entry closes:** we are still at the entrance. The [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/Events/Missing-Riders|riders are still missing]], and the [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/Affairs/Wagon-Escort-Job|wagon is still undelivered]].
