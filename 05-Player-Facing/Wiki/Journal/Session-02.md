# Journal: Into the Forest Cave

*Written by one of the hired escorts, inside the cave.*

**In-world date:** 15 Tarsakh, 1491 DR

Skarn went into the [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/Locations/Forest-Cave|Forest Cave]] and intimidated the [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/People/Cave-Wolves|wolves]], stopping them briefly. Farther inside, the goblins released a rush of water through the passage. None of us was swept away. Fighting followed against goblins and wolves. A second flood struck Groot Thornmark and KraziKatt, but both stood fast.

We won the fight deeper inside the cave. We were hurt, but none of us fell there. As Tyr Asirafell approached the next room, a goblin's arrow struck him. Skarn rushed the [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/People/Cave-Bugbear|bugbear]] inside and hit him, but was badly wounded during the fight. KraziKatt killed the bugbear with *Guiding Bolt*.

One goblin escaped down a trash chute. Another knocked Tyr down before being felled. KraziKatt stabilized Tyr, and Groot healed him with *Goodberry*.

Searching the containers, we noticed a [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/Lore/Blue-Lion-Mark|blue lion mark]]. Tyr broke off a marked piece of a container to carry with him. We left the containers behind and took a short rest in the room.

Afterwards we crossed the bridge to another goblin cave. Rolph scouted ahead, saw a [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/People/Goblins|goblin boss]], and returned to warn Tyr. Tyr advanced and fired an arrow, but missed.

**As this entry closes:** the fight has started in the goblin cave beyond the bridge; the fight has not yet been resolved. We have taken only the broken-off mark, not the containers. The [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/Events/Missing-Riders|missing riders]] remain unaccounted for, and we have not reached [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/Locations/Phandalin|Phandalin]].
