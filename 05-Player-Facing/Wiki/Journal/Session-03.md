# Journal: Sildar Survives

**Date played:** 2026-11-05
**In-world date:** The encounter continued on 15 Tarsakh, 1491 DR. The long rest in the cave advanced the in-game day by one, to 16 Tarsakh.

The fight in the goblin den beyond the bridge in the [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/Locations/Forest-Cave|Forest Cave]] was far from over, and it did not stay easy for long. In the corridor, things went our way. Then Tyr stepped into the room, and a goblin boss cut him down almost before he had drawn breath. Skarn charged in after him and tore through the smaller [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/People/Goblins|goblins]], one after another.

A second boss dragged [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/People/Sildar-Hallwinter|Sildar]] up the stairs and dangled him over the edge, calling for a parley. We would not bargain. The goblin let go, and Sildar fell. He came very close to dying, but he clung to life. Rolph met both bosses head-on and went down as well. The battle hung in the balance until the group finally killed both bosses. When the dust settled, we pulled Tyr and Rolph back to their feet.

Exhausted, we took a long rest in the den, and we woke stronger: level 2.

Sildar, still shaken, spoke about [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/People/Elongated-Head-Goblin|a goblin with an elongated head]] and [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/Locations/Cragmaw-Castle|Cragmaw Castle]]. He also mentioned [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/Lore/Gundrens-Map|Gundren's map]], but we did not ask further about it. He asked us to escort him to [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/Locations/Phandalin|Phandalin]], and we agreed.

**Where we stopped:** After speaking with Sildar, we left the cave and hurried to check on the wagon, oxen and horses. To our relief, everything was there and unharmed. We set out for Phandalin and halted at its edge, just short of the first buildings. The containers left in the cave have not been recovered; only the broken-off piece bearing the [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/Lore/Blue-Lion-Mark|blue lion mark]] has been carried away. [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/People/Gundren-Rockseeker|Gundren]] and his map remain missing.
