# The Wagon Escort Job

[[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/People/Gundren-Rockseeker|Gundren Rockseeker]] hired a company of escorts in [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/Locations/Neverwinter|Neverwinter]] to drive and guard his ox-drawn supply wagon to [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/Locations/Barthens-Provisions|Barthen's Provisions]] in Phandalin.

> [!note] Incomplete entry
> The outcome of this job is not yet known. Those with firsthand knowledge are encouraged to add to this entry.

**Agreed payment:** Ten gold pieces per escort upon safe delivery. Gundren and [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/People/Sildar-Hallwinter|Sildar]] rode ahead.

**Latest word:** The escorts left the wagon's animals tethered at the roadside while they looked into the riders' disappearance. The wagon has not reached Phandalin, and no payment has been made.

**Unsettled:** Nobody yet knows how the escorts will keep their commitment to the delivery while the riders remain missing.

## The Notice
*A crisp parchment sheet pinned with an iron tack to the bulletin boards of the Moonstone Mask, the Driftwood Tavern, and the South Gate trade yards in Neverwinter.*

```text
======================================================================
                   STURDY HANDS & GUARDS WANTED!
             PROVISIONS ESCORT SOUTH TO PHANDALIN
======================================================================

Looking for reliable sellswords, wanderers, or capable escorts to 
shepherd a laden wagon of provisions, dry stores, mining tackle, and 
fresh ale south along the High Road, then east along the Triboar Trail 
to the settlement of Phandalin.

CARGO: 
Two stout oxen, a covered wagon packed with flour, salted pork, ale, 
pickaxes, crowbars, lanterns, and lamp oil.

PAYMENT: 
Ten gold pieces (10 gp) per head, disbursed in hard coin upon safe, 
undamaged delivery of the wagon and cargo to Barthen's Provisions in 
Phandalin.

DETAILS: 
The road south can be rough and goblin raiders are not unheard of in 
the foothills. You will travel together with the wagon at walking pace 
(approx. 3–4 days). I and my companion, the knight Sildar Hallwinter, 
ride ahead on fast horses to secure business, permits, and lodgings.

DEPARTURE: 
South Gate Caravan Grounds, Neverwinter. Wagon harnessed at dawn. 
Ask the yardmaster for the Rockseeker freight.

"Fair coin for fair labor. See you at the bottom of a cold flagon."

                       — Gundren Rockseeker
                         Clan Rockseeker, Prospector & Merchant
======================================================================
```

## What the Job Offered
- **Immediate cash:** 10 gold pieces waiting at Barthen's Provisions in Phandalin.
- **Supplies on the road:** Free passage with the wagon, with space to stow bedrolls and gear.
- **A foot in the door:** A connection to a passionate dwarven prospector who insists he and his brothers are on the verge of "something grand and epoch-making."
