# Blue Lion Mark

A blue lion mark appeared on containers the escorts searched in the [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/Locations/Forest-Cave|Forest Cave]] after defeating the [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/People/Cave-Bugbear|bugbear]]. They did not identify what the mark represents.

Tyr broke off part of a container so he could carry the mark. The containers themselves were left behind, as recounted in the [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/Journal/Session-02|second journal entry]].
