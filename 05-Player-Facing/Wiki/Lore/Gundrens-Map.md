# Gundren's Map

[[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/People/Sildar-Hallwinter|Sildar]] mentioned a map belonging to [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/People/Gundren-Rockseeker|Gundren]] after the den fight. The party did not ask further about it.

Gundren and his map remain missing. The [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/Journal/Session-03|third journal entry]] records the mention; it does not establish what the map shows or who now holds it.
