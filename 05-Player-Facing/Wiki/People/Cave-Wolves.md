# Cave Wolves

Wolves were encountered inside the [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/Locations/Forest-Cave|Forest Cave]], first near the entrance and later in the fighting deeper within.

Skarn intimidated the wolves just inside, briefly stopping them. The escorts later fought wolves as well as [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/People/Goblins|goblins]] while moving through the cave.

The [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/Journal/Session-02|second journal entry]] recounts the fighting and the floods.
