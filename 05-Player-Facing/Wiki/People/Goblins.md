# Goblins

> [!note] Incomplete entry
> The escorts have fought these goblins on the road and inside the cave, but their purposes remain unknown.

Goblins have attacked travellers at a wooded bend on the [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/Locations/Triboar-Trail|Triboar Trail]], outside the [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/Locations/Forest-Cave|Forest Cave]], and within it. Gundren Rockseeker's notice itself warned that goblin raiders are not unheard of in the foothills.

Across much of Faerûn, goblins meet suspicion rather than the ready acceptance extended to many other unfamiliar folk. That widespread distrust is not knowledge of this particular band's purposes.

Four sprang the roadside ambush from the brush. Three fell; the fourth escaped into the woods despite Skarn's pursuit. The cave lookouts also attacked and were defeated.

Inside the cave, goblins released two floods. The escorts fought goblins and wolves deeper inside, then fought a [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/People/Cave-Bugbear|bugbear]] and goblins in another room. One goblin escaped down a trash chute; another was felled after knocking Tyr down.

After crossing the bridge, Rolph spotted a goblin boss in another cave and returned to warn Tyr. Tyr's arrow missed; initiative has been rolled, with the encounter still unresolved. The escorts do not know what these goblins call themselves or what they want.
