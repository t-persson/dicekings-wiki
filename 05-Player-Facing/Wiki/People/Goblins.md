# Goblins

> [!note] Incomplete entry
> Little is known of these goblins beyond two attacks. Those with firsthand knowledge are encouraged to add to this entry.

Goblins have attacked travellers twice: at a wooded bend on the [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/Locations/Triboar-Trail|Triboar Trail]] and outside the [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/Locations/Forest-Cave|Forest Cave]]. Gundren Rockseeker's notice itself warned that goblin raiders are not unheard of in the foothills.

Across much of Faerûn, goblins meet suspicion rather than the ready acceptance extended to many other unfamiliar folk. That widespread distrust is not knowledge of this particular band's purposes.

Four sprang the roadside ambush from the brush. Three fell; the fourth escaped into the woods despite Skarn's pursuit. The cave lookouts also attacked and were defeated.

Both attacks used concealment beside the road and the cave. Nobody yet knows what these goblins call themselves, who leads them, or what they want.
