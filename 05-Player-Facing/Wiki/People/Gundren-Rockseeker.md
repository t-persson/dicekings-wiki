# Gundren Rockseeker

Gundren is the dwarf who hired escorts in [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/Locations/Neverwinter|Neverwinter]] to bring a supply wagon to [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/Locations/Phandalin|Phandalin]]. He offered ten gold pieces each for safe delivery.

He rode ahead with [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/People/Sildar-Hallwinter|Sildar Hallwinter]], leaving the wagon in the escorts' care.

> [!note] Incomplete entry
> The escorts know Gundren from meeting him in Neverwinter and accepting his job. Those who know more are encouraged to add to this entry.

The escorts do not know where Gundren is now. At the ambush site, a horse said that Sildar and "the dwarf" had been taken into the forest; that testimony is a lead, not confirmation of where Gundren is.
