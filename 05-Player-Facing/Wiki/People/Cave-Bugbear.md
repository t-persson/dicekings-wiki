# The Cave Bugbear

A bugbear fought the escorts in a room inside the [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/Locations/Forest-Cave|Forest Cave]]. KraziKatt killed him with *Guiding Bolt*.

Skarn rushed him and landed a blow, but was badly wounded during the fight. Tyr was knocked down by a goblin before KraziKatt stabilized him and Groot healed him with *Goodberry*.

The escorts searched containers bearing a [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/Lore/Blue-Lion-Mark|blue lion mark]] and took a short rest in the room. Their account is in the [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/Journal/Session-02|second journal entry]].
