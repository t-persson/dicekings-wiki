# Sildar Hallwinter

> [!note] Incomplete entry
> This entry is thin. Those who know Sildar are encouraged to add to it.

Sildar is the warrior travelling with [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/People/Gundren-Rockseeker|Gundren Rockseeker]]. Gundren's job notice names him as his companion; the two rode ahead toward [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/Locations/Phandalin|Phandalin]].

When Groot questioned the [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/People/Riderless-Horses|riderless horses]], one named Sildar and said that he and a dwarf had been taken into the forest.

The escorts followed that lead but have not found Sildar. They do not know where he is now, or how he fares.
