# Sildar Hallwinter

> [!note] Incomplete entry
> Sildar has been rescued. His reports about the castle, goblin and map leave questions to follow up.

Sildar is the warrior travelling with [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/People/Gundren-Rockseeker|Gundren Rockseeker]]. Gundren's job notice names him as his companion; the two rode ahead toward [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/Locations/Phandalin|Phandalin]].

When Groot questioned the [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/People/Riderless-Horses|riderless horses]], one named Sildar and said that he and a dwarf had been taken into the forest.

The escorts found him in the goblin den inside the [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/Locations/Forest-Cave|Forest Cave]]. A goblin boss held him over a ledge and tried to parley; when the party ignored the attempt, the boss dropped him. Sildar nearly died, but survived the fight.

Afterwards he spoke about [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/People/Elongated-Head-Goblin|a goblin with an elongated head]] and [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/Locations/Cragmaw-Castle|Cragmaw Castle]]. He mentioned [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/Lore/Gundrens-Map|Gundren's map]], but the party did not ask further about it.

He asked the escorts to take him to Phandalin, and they agreed. The [[05-Player-Facing/Wiki/Journal/Session-03|third journal entry]] records his rescue and the agreement; the company stopped just before entering town.
